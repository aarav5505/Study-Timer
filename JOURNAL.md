## November 7, 2026: Learning circuits in Tinkercad

I started learning electronics using Tinkercads circuit tutorials to prepare for my study-timer. I practiced placing components, wiring them, and understanding which breadboard holes connect together. In the RGB LED circuit , I connected the resistor the positive rail instead of the negative rail and learned how that prevented it from lighting. I also explored the Aruino blink example and how turning an output HIGH or LOW controls an LED. Next, I want to apply these basics to my timers actual digital circuit.
